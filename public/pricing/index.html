Good to know

Questions, answered.

Anything else on your mind? Just ask — I’m happy to help.

Do I need to know how to pose?

Not at all — that’s my job. I’ll guide you through where to stand, what to do with your hands and how to move, and give you direction whenever you need it.

How many photos will I receive?

Portrait & Lifestyle sessions include 15 favorites that you choose for detailed editing, plus the remaining usable proof images. Wedding and elopement packages list their edited image counts on the pricing page.

How do I choose my 15 favorites?

After your portrait session you’ll receive a private online proof gallery. Just tap the heart on the photographs you love most, and those 15 receive my detailed editing.

Where do sessions take place?

Anywhere around Denver, Littleton and the Front Range — parks, foothills, downtown, or your own home. I’m happy to recommend locations with beautiful light for the look you want.

Do you travel?

Yes. Travel fees may apply outside the Denver area, Colorado mountain weddings 50+ miles from Littleton are +$500, and destination weddings are priced based on travel.

How do I reserve my date?

A retainer reserves your date. For portrait sessions the remainder is due on the day of your session; for weddings and elopements the balance is due one week before the wedding.

What should we wear?

I’ll share styling guidance when we plan your session. In general, coordinated (not matching) colors, comfortable clothes you feel great in and a few textures photograph beautifully.
