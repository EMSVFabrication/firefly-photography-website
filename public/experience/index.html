The Firefly experience

Beautiful photographs begin with feeling comfortable enough to be yourself.

Being in front of a camera doesn’t come naturally to everyone. That’s where I come in.

I’ll guide you through the process, find the beautiful light, help with the little details, and give you direction when you need it. You don’t have to know what to do with your hands or how to pose. That’s my job.

Along the way, we’ll laugh, move around, try things, and leave room for the moments nobody could have planned.

My approach blends thoughtful direction with genuine connection, creating photographs that feel polished and beautiful without losing what makes them yours.

The goal isn’t simply to look incredible in your photographs. It’s to feel comfortable, confident, and genuinely enjoy creating them.

The process

From the First Hello to Your Final Gallery

Easy, enjoyable and organized — so you always know what comes next.

Families, seniors, maternity, newborns, headshots and other portrait packages.

  1. 01

    Inquiry & planning

    You tell me about the session you’re envisioning. We talk through the location, timing, overall look and any special requests, and I’ll recommend beautiful Colorado locations and share preparation and styling guidance.

  2. 02

    The photography session

    We meet at the location and I guide you through everything — posing, natural movement, flattering angles and light, and comfortable interactions. We capture both thoughtfully composed portraits and candid moments.

  3. 03

    Your proof gallery

    After the session I review every frame and prepare a private online proof gallery with all the usable images, so you can see everything we captured.

  4. 04

    Choosing your favorites

    You select your 15 favorite photographs for detailed editing, simply by tapping the heart on the ones you love. You choose the images that matter most to you — I don’t make every final decision for you.

  5. 05

    Detailed editing & delivery

    Your 15 favorites receive my full, detailed editing treatment. You also receive the remaining usable proof images as part of your package — a broad collection of memories alongside a carefully refined collection of favorite portraits.

Weddings and elopements follow their own process — the 15-favorites selection doesn’t apply.

  1. 01

    Inquiry & consultation

    We talk through your date, venue, guest count and the feel you want your photographs to have, and find the coverage that fits.

  2. 02

    Reserve your date

    A retainer holds your date; the balance is due one week before the wedding.

  3. 03

    Engagement session

    Full wedding packages include a one-hour engagement session at Mt. Falcon Park — a relaxed way to get comfortable in front of the camera together.

  4. 04

    Planning the day

    We map out the timeline, family photo list and the best light for portraits, so the day feels calm and nothing important is missed.

  5. 05

    Your wedding day

    Storytelling coverage from beginning to end, with gentle direction for portraits and an eye for every candid moment.

  6. 06

    Your full gallery

    Your edited gallery is delivered within six weeks — or in two weeks with the rush delivery add-on.

Good to know

Questions, answered.

Anything else on your mind? Just ask — I’m happy to help.

Do I need to know how to pose?

Not at all — that’s my job. I’ll guide you through where to stand, what to do with your hands and how to move, and give you direction whenever you need it.

How many photos will I receive?

Portrait & Lifestyle sessions include 15 favorites that you choose for detailed editing, plus the remaining usable proof images. Wedding and elopement packages list their edited image counts on the pricing page.

How do I choose my 15 favorites?

After your portrait session you’ll receive a private online proof gallery. Just tap the heart on the photographs you love most, and those 15 receive my detailed editing.

Where do sessions take place?

Anywhere around Denver, Littleton and the Front Range — parks, foothills, downtown, or your own home. I’m happy to recommend locations with beautiful light for the look you want.

Do you travel?

Yes. Travel fees may apply outside the Denver area, Colorado mountain weddings 50+ miles from Littleton are +$500, and destination weddings are priced based on travel.

How do I reserve my date?

A retainer reserves your date. For portrait sessions the remainder is due on the day of your session; for weddings and elopements the balance is due one week before the wedding.

What should we wear?

I’ll share styling guidance when we plan your session. In general, coordinated (not matching) colors, comfortable clothes you feel great in and a few textures photograph beautifully.
