The Firefly experience

Beautiful photographs begin with feeling comfortable enough to be yourself.

Being in front of a camera doesn’t come naturally to everyone. That’s where I come in.

I’ll guide you through the process, find the beautiful light, help with the little details, and give you direction when you need it. You don’t have to know what to do with your hands or how to pose. That’s my job.

Explore the experience
  • 01

    Guided, never stiff

    Posing, hands, angles, where to look — I direct the details so you can stop worrying and simply be there.

  • 02

    Chasing beautiful light

    I plan locations and timing around the most flattering light, from Colorado golden hour to soft window light at home.

  • 03

    Room for real moments

    Between the composed portraits, we laugh, move and play — and that’s often where the favorite photographs happen.

  • 04

    Organized from hello to gallery

    Clear planning, an easy proof gallery and carefully finished images. You always know what comes next.

Beautiful photography isn’t just about how a moment looks. It’s about remembering how it felt.
